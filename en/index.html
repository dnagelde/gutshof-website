Frequently asked questions

Answered briefly and concretely.

How many people can stay?

Around 60 beds in 18 apartments across three buildings on one shared estate. A complete crew stays together at one address, including larger teams.

What does accommodation cost?

25 to 30 euros net per person per night, depending on occupancy, duration and season, plus VAT. You normally receive a binding quote the same working day.

What is the minimum booking period?

By the week. Periods follow your project schedule, and extending is straightforward at any time.

How is it invoiced?

One invoice for the whole block booking with VAT shown separately, to one contact. No administering individual bookings.

Can the crew cook for themselves?

Yes. Every apartment has a fully equipped kitchen with oven, ceramic hob, dishwasher, coffee machine, fridge and freezer. That is considerably cheaper than hotel and restaurant.

Is there parking for vans?

Yes, free parking right on the yard, including Sprinters and larger company vehicles. Five hectares of grounds leave room for vehicles and materials.

How does arrival work?

By self check-in via key box. You receive the code by e-mail before arrival, no handover appointment is needed. Arrival from 4 pm, departure by 10 am.

Can laundry be washed?

Yes, washing machines are available on site. They run on a coin machine, 2 euros per 4 hours.

How far is it to the region's building sites?

Stralsund 18 km (about 20 minutes), Grimmen 20 km (23 minutes), Greifswald 30 km (40 minutes), Wolgast 60 km, Sassnitz on Rügen 65 km.

How short-notice can a booking be?

Short-notice bookings are possible subject to availability. Call us on 0049 3831 6153005 or use the enquiry form.
