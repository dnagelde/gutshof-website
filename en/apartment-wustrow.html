The apartment

The „Wustrow“ occupies two floors in the manor's brick building, quietly located on the enclosed grounds. Three separate bedrooms sleep up to seven people.

The ground floor holds a bedroom with a television and its own bathroom with shower, plus the terrace. Upstairs are the fully equipped kitchen with dining area – the meeting point in the evening – two further bedrooms and the bathroom with bathtub.

Layout

  • Upper floor, room 1: three single beds (0.90 × 2.00 m)
  • Upper floor, room 2: three single beds (0.90 × 2.00 m)
  • Upper floor: kitchen with dining area, bathroom with bathtub
  • Outside: terrace with seating outdoors
